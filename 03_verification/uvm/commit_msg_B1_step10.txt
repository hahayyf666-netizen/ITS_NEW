B1: judge the tail on the pin, anchored to the run's own end

The tail_stress case already drives the window and checks the TU output; what it
did not do was judge the boundary the grant contract is about.  It does now, on
the same run, without a new test: the pin record is tapped by the existing
+GRANT_AUDIT hook and the case names its contract.

What is checked, and why each part is needed:

  - one applied profile and one 600-edge transaction, the profile's own
    parameters compared with the log line (always=0 light=7/11 deep=1/600
    dur=600), so an edited profile cannot pass as this run's;
  - the window: 600 edges from the first valid edge, all req=0, completing at
    1,207,000 ps with finish_item() returning at the same timestamp;
  - E, the 600th executing edge, must still be driving (req=0 at E): the
    contract counts executed edges, so expiry lands after that edge, and the
    edge itself is part of the evidence;
  - from E+1 to the last sampling edge every row must be rst_n=1 and req=1.  No
    minimum length: the user's reading of the contract is that the guard runs to
    the end of the run, and the length is whatever the run makes it;
  - the first guard edge must be exactly one clock after E, and the last
    sampling edge must equal the run's own TEST_DONE timestamp.  The TEST_DONE
    anchor is what makes a truncated recording visible: the remaining edges stay
    contiguous and stay high, but they no longer reach the end of the run.

Measured on the real DUT, and identical to the independent run of c011fb2:

  window      600 edges from t=9000, all req=0, ends t=1207000
  guard       first edge t=1209000 (one clock after E), last t=14073000
  tail        6,433 edges, every one rst_n=1 req=1
  TEST_DONE   t=14073000, matching the last sampling edge
  CSV         7,037 rows, one per clock, 4 of them the startup reset

The 6,433 is recorded as this run's measurement, not as a constant: the judge
computes the expected tail from TEST_DONE each time.

Five mutants on the real record, all rejected: the first guard edge low, a guard
edge in the middle low, the last guard edge low, the recording cut by 40 edges
with TEST_DONE kept, and the window's last edge raised to req=1.

Also fixed in this commit: the report-source check compared with endswith, so a

One observation about the stimulus, recorded rather than changed: the profile log
says deep=1/600, and deep_period=1 is outside the item's randomised range of
[8:128].  The sequence sets it through its own single-shot fields, which is how
600 consecutive low edges are produced.  The contract therefore states the
profile exactly as the driver logs it instead of re-deriving it from the item's
constraints.
