B1: tie the duration rejection to the item it refused

The independent audit found four records that judge_dur() accepted although the
contract they claim to satisfy does not hold.  Each one is a place where the
judgement read a weaker fact than the claim:

  1. the report was only counted, never located - moving the ITS_GRANT_DUR stamp
     to the end of the second window kept failures empty.  "That id occurred
     once somewhere" and "the driver raised it while refusing this item" are
     different claims; the branch reports synchronously, so both the component
     and the timestamp are now checked against the item;
  2. the three items had no order - moving the rejected item one cycle ahead of
     the first window's completion kept end == begin, and the guard stretch even
     grew to 20 edges.  Now window[0].end <= rejected.begin <= rejected.end <=
     window[1].begin, with equality allowed because a waiting driver hands over
     in a zero-cycle handshake;
  3. the guard stretch was filtered on rst_n before counting - flipping one row
     to rst_n=0, req=0 removed it from the count and 18 edges still cleared the
     floor of 15.  Every row in the stretch is now inspected for both signals,
     which is also what the case's own contract says: no reset during guard;
  4. the two legal windows never had their declared durations checked - editing
     the second window's begin record to dur=17 while it ran 100 edges passed,
     because only the rejected item's records were compared.  Both sides of
     every item are now compared with the contract, as the three-window judge
     already did.
