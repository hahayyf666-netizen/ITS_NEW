B1: count rows only when the clock agrees, and keep the cause of a failure

Three defects found by replaying records against the judge, each fixed at the
place it can be proved rather than at the place it was noticed.

1. A duplicated row passed as an extra sampling edge.  Replacing the cyc=130
   row with a copy of cyc=129 left the contract reporting reset_runs=[(24,4)]
   and failures=[]  - eight reset rows carrying seven distinct instants, three
   in the target window.  Counting rows is only counting edges if the
   timestamps say so, so the judge now requires one row per clock before it
   looks at the waveform, and rejects repeated instants outright.  This also
   guards the length measurements B1 still has to make: a length stated in
   edges is worthless if rows can be duplicated or dropped.  Two mutants.

2. The judge's own source was not an input.  b1_grant_audit.py and
   b1_pin_model.py decide PASS/FAIL and were absent from FINGERPRINT_FILES, so
   a stored result could survive a change to the thing that judged it.  Both
   are in the fingerprint now, and a case with a directed judgement records the
   sha256 of the waveform CSV and the log it was read from, because the .result
   digest alone does not say which bytes were examined.

3. The directed hook hid why a case had failed.  classify() ran first, then any
   directed finding overwrote status/reason with FAIL/DIRECTED - and "the
   underlying verdict is not PASS" is itself one of those findings.  Driving a
   timed-out run through the hook did exactly that.  apply_directed() now keeps
   the underlying status and reason and appends the waveform findings, dropping
   the verdict echo that duplicated the cause; only a case that passed on its
   own and disagrees with the waveform is attributed to the waveform.  Two
   checks in the self-test, one per direction.

Measured after the change:

  timeout + no evidence      INCOMPLETE/TIMEOUT, waveform findings appended
  passed case, bad waveform  FAIL/DIRECTED
  regression                 23/23 PASS, functional coverage 52/54
  fingerprint                19fddf6bef8ba9ca (moves: the judge is an input now)
  self-test                  20 mutants caught, 2 attributions kept, agreed
                             record accepted

Direction of travel: these are checks about the checker, so the honest claim is
that the judge now refuses a record it used to accept, not that the DUT behaves
better.  deep_len boundaries, ITS_GRANT_DUR and the tail_stress expiry are next,
and the timestamp rule is what makes an edge count usable as a length.
