B1: deep_len at both ends of its legal range, judged by position

Four windows in one run, deep_len 1, 2, 15 and 16 - the constrained range is
[1:16], so this is the lower bound, the upper bound and one interior point on
each side.  deep_period is 64 throughout, which matters: cyc is global, so with
the first edge at cyc=4 and one guard edge between windows the six multiples of
64 inside the four windows are 64, 128, 192, 256, 320 and 384, and two of the
windows see two stalls each.

Parameters.  light_mod=10, not 12.  At cyc=192 a two-cycle stall ends on 193 and
194 % 12 == 2 would put a light stall on the release cycle: the pin would be low
for three cycles while the driver is still correct, and the criterion would call
it a failure.  With 10/13 every first and release cycle of the six stalls was
checked by hand and is clear of the light rule.

Criterion.  Positions, not lengths found in the recording: for each agreed
trigger T, req[T-1] is 1, req[T..T+deep_len-1] are all 0, and req[T+deep_len] is
1.  The last two separate "too short" from "too long"; the first catches a stall
that starts a cycle late.  Checking each trigger rather than "at least one stall
per window" is what makes a dropped second stall visible - window 1 would still
have passed on its cyc=128 stall alone.  An independent scan then collects low
runs that begin on a deep-period boundary and requires the set to be exactly the
six agreed cycles, so an invented stall fails too.

Measured on the real DUT (fingerprint 1044026b0cf11637):

  deep_len  window  T    T-1  low interval    first high
  1         0       64   1    64-64           65
  2         1       128  1    128-129         130
  2         1       192  1    192-193         194
  15        2       256  1    256-270         271
  16        3       320  1    320-335         336
  16        3       384  1    384-399         400

The independent scan returns exactly [64, 128, 192, 256, 320, 384].  Low-run
widths across the four windows are 49x1, 8x2, 1x15 and 2x16, which is why the
criterion cannot be "find a run of the right length": eight unrelated cycles
already produce two-cycle lows.

Mutants, all rejected without a simulator: one cycle short at deep_len=1 (the
trigger cycle high - no need to send deep_len=0, which the constraint does not
allow), one cycle short at deep_len=16, one cycle long at cyc=192, and window 1
never issuing its second stall.  The agreed record is accepted.

adds a check, not a bin.
